HS Realty Research
About this project
This is my beginner real estate research project focused on Ahmedabad, Gujarat.
My goal is to understand how property markets work and learn to identify potentially undervalued locations before they become popular.

Research location

Vejalpur, Ahmedabad

What I am studying

- Property prices
- Infrastructure development
- Connectivity and transportation
- Residential demand
- Rental potential
- Property appreciation
- Investment risks

My research approach
I will compare different neighbourhoods, study available property listings, investigate infrastructure projects and record my findings.
I will distinguish between asking prices and actual transaction prices.

Project status

Learning and research in progress.

*This is an educational research project, not financial or investment advice.*
